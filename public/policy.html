Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Webore ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at webore.studio or use our services.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number when you fill out our contact form.
  • Account data — first name, last name, email, and hashed password when you register for an account.
  • Project information — details you share about your project, budget, and requirements.
  • OAuth data — if you log in via Google or GitHub, we receive your public profile information (name, email, avatar) from those providers.
  • Communications — messages you send through our AI chat widget.

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our site:

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent)
  • Device information (operating system, screen resolution)
  • Cookie and session data for authentication purposes

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your project inquiries and provide our services
  • Create and manage your account
  • Send you updates about your project status
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

4. Cookies & Sessions

We use HTTP-only session cookies strictly for authentication purposes. These cookies allow you to stay logged in and access your dashboard. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, but doing so may prevent you from using authenticated features of our site.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a SQLite database (development) or equivalent secure relational database (production) hosted on infrastructure we control. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored in plain text.

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS/TLS encryption, HTTP-only cookies, CSRF protection, rate limiting, and input sanitization. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google OAuth — for social login (Google Privacy Policy applies)
  • GitHub OAuth — for social login (GitHub Privacy Policy applies)
  • Google Fonts — typography loaded from Google's CDN
  • Groq / AI Provider — for powering our chat assistant (API key calls, no user data retained)

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at webore1007@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
